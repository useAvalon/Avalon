## Short answer

Both build HTML-first sites (blogs, docs, marketing) with islands for the bits that need to be interactive.

Astro pages are `.astro`. Astro includes [content collections](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/) to query files as typed data.

Avalon pages are JSX or MDX. Avalon includes [Nitro](https://nitro.build) so APIs, middleware, and deploy presets sit next to those pages.

## What is similar

- **Islands:** server-rendered HTML first; interactive regions hydrate on the client.
- **Content-heavy sites:** landers, blogs, docs, and guides with occasional widgets.
- **File-based routing** and **MDX**.

## Where they diverge

| Dimension | Astro | Avalon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Page authoring** | `.astro` templates as the default page format | JSX and MDX pages and layouts (`.tsx` / `.mdx`) |
| **Typed content in the repo** | [Content collections](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/): schemas, `getCollection`, `getEntry` from a `src/content` tree | No first-party collection API. Content is **routes** (`.tsx` / `.mdx` under [modules](/docs/modules) or `src/pages/`), **imports**, or data you [load at build or request time](/docs/guides/data-loading) |
| **Server / deployment** | Static output by default; server and on-demand output through [deployment adapters](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/deploy/) (for example [`@astrojs/node`](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/integrations-guide/node/)) | [**Nitro**](https://nitro.build) presets plus [API routes](/docs/api-routes), [middleware](/docs/middleware), [server actions](/docs/server-actions), and [cron jobs](/docs/cron-jobs) |
| **Skills and ecosystem** | [Integrations directory](https://astro.build/integrations/) and tutorials centered on **Astro** APIs | **Vite**, **JSX**, and **Nitro** skills that carry over from other app stacks |

## When Astro is the better fit

- Your team wants to **author pages in `.astro`** and embed UI frameworks from that template language.
- You want Astro’s **content collections** to validate, list, and query markdown/MDX as data, not only as routable pages.
- You plan to rely on Astro’s **integrations directory** and Astro-oriented guides for setup and deployment.

## When Avalon is the better fit

- You want **JSX and MDX** pages and layouts for marketing, docs, and product UI in one authoring style.
- You want **Nitro** as the default server and deployment story for APIs, middleware, and presets alongside those pages.
- You ship **interactive product UI** in the **same repo** as marketing and docs, with JSX, MDX, and Nitro end to end.
- Your team already works in **Vite + JSX + Nitro** and wants islands without adopting `.astro` or Astro-only content APIs.

## What this guide is not

- A benchmark. No “faster,” “smaller,” or “better SEO” claim. Measure your routes.
- A migration recipe.
- An independent review. Check [Astro’s docs](https://docs.astro.build).
